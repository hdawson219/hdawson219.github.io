Privacy Policy for Hermes Job Search Agent

Effective date: 2026-10-26

Application name: Hermes Job Search Agent
Contact: hdawson219@gmail.com

1. Data we access

This application accesses the following Google APIs on behalf of its owner, using OAuth 2.0 with explicit consent:

  • Gmail (read, send, modify) — to search job alert emails and send recruiter outreach drafts
  • Google Calendar — to read calendar events for scheduling
  • Google Drive — to read/write job-tracker spreadsheets and backup files
  • Google Sheets — to read/write job search tracking spreadsheets
  • Google Docs — to read/write job descriptions and notes
  • Google Contacts (read-only) — to find warm connections at target companies

2. How we use your data

All API calls are made only at the owner's direction (via scheduled cron jobs or manual commands they initiate). Data is used solely to automate a personal job search: finding postings, filtering by criteria, and writing results to the owner's private Google Sheets. No data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers are collected.

3. Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine. Job data is stored in the owner's private Google Sheets. No external database or server stores this data.

4. Your rights

Access may be revoked at any time via Google Account Permissions.

5. Changes

Updates will be reflected here with a new effective date.
